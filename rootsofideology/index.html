The Two Foundations of Society

Use the handy memory acronyms P.R.I.C.E.S. and C.C.C.P.E.N. to remember the key values!

🧍

Individualism

Focus: The Individual First

P.R.I.C.E.S.

Emphasizes personal freedom, self-reliance, and the idea that society thrives best when individuals pursue their own goals without excessive government interference.

🔑 Private Property

The legal right to own land, houses, businesses, and belongings personally.

⚖️ Rule of Law

Every citizen is subject to the same laws—no leader or wealthy person is above the law.

🗽 Individual Rights & Freedoms

Protected rights such as free speech, freedom of religion, and democratic voting.

📈 Competition

Rivalry between producers encourages innovation, better products, and lower prices.

💼 Economic Freedom

The freedom to buy, sell, trade, and choose your occupation without strict control.

💪 Self-Reliance

Taking personal responsibility for one's own success, wellbeing, and hard work.

🤝

Collectivism

Focus: The Group / Common Good

C.C.C.P.E.N.

Emphasizes human interdependence, community strength, and working collaboratively to ensure the security and welfare of all community members.

🎯 Collective Interest

Prioritizing goals that benefit the whole group over individual personal desires.

🛡️ Collective Responsibility

Holding the group accountable for caring for all members (e.g., healthcare, safety nets).

🤝 Cooperation

Working together harmoniously toward shared goals rather than competing.

🏛️ Public Property

Shared ownership of key resources like public parks, state roads, and schools.

📊 Economic Equality

Policies like progressive taxation to reduce income gaps and ensure basic needs for all.

📜 Norms & Adherence

Shared social expectations, customs, and standards that maintain group unity.
