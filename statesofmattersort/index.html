Teacher notes & credits

Focus: shape, volume and a simple particle model. Compare the same material in different states.

Particle dots are schematic, not to scale; they show arrangement, not actual particle size or colour. All particles move: solid particles vibrate about fixed positions. The gas cards use a particle symbol because these gases are invisible.

Do not confuse visible clouds or steam mist (liquid droplets) with invisible water vapour.
