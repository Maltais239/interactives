Why these choices?
    A good question to talk about

    Could a want become a need? In this sort, a bike is for recreation, and TV and games are for entertainment. A bike used to get to school, a comfort toy, or a device used to communicate could be much more important in someone else’s situation. Invite students to explain their reasoning.

    Our rule: basic daily needs include nutritious food, clean water, suitable clothing, shelter, clean air, health care and education. The pictured extras are wants in this example.
