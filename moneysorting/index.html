How to play · For students and teachers

Build the target using any combination of Canadian money. You can drag bills and coins, tap them to add, or use Tab then Enter/Space with your keyboard. Tap a piece in the tray to remove it. All amounts are in multiples of 5¢, as used for Canadian cash payments. You can use more than one of the same coin or bill.
