Good to know

  • Vatican: cover shoulders and knees. No photos or phone use in the Sistine Chapel.
  • Water: Rome’s small nasoni fountains supply drinking water. Refill a bottle; observe any posted restrictions.
  • Thursday: Pantheon → Trevi → Spanish Steps makes a compact central walk. Use maps for the live route.
  • Florence: Piazzale Michelangelo is uphill, south of the Arno. Leave time for the climb and the trip back to the station.
  • Trains: trust the station, train number, departure time and carriage on your ticket. The platform (binario) may appear later on the board.
