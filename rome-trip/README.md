# Rome travel companion

Live app: https://maltais239.github.io/interactives/rome-trip/

Public landmark recognition and Italian practice: 42 photographed places across eight destinations, 95 speaking challenges across 19 situations, short history cards, maps, and browser-local notes. No personal travel itinerary, travel dates, accommodation address, or booking details are included.

Audio uses an installed Italian device voice. Landmark cards include photographer credits and source links.

Menu practice uses published offerings from Il Gianfornaio (Prati), L’Archetto, La Zanzara, Gelateria dei Gracchi, Sciascia Caffè 1919 and Panino Divino, plus 200Gradi examples published by Rome’s official tourism office. All seven menus have food-and-drink or gelato phrase builders, five reply challenges, original dialogues, useful requests and source/map links. Food choices focus on meat, cheese and dairy desserts, with no vegan food options. Menu sources were checked 9–10 October 2026; availability can change. Drink and takeaway selections are practice requests, not guarantees of current service. Practice text is included in the versioned offline cache.
