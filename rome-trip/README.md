# Rome travel companion

Live app: https://maltais239.github.io/interactives/rome-trip/

Public landmark recognition and Italian practice: 42 photographed places across eight destinations, 60 speaking challenges across 12 situations, short history cards, maps, and browser-local notes. No personal travel itinerary, travel dates, accommodation address, or booking details are included.

Audio uses an installed Italian device voice. Landmark cards include photographer credits and source links.
