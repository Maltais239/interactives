Sort six animals by their usual wild diets. What evidence supports each choice?

Teacher notes & credits

Learning focus: classify using evidence, explain a choice, then revise. Each challenge includes two herbivores, two carnivores and two omnivores.

Before the sort: invite students to predict. Reveal field notes when they need evidence, then check their sort. Ask partners to explain one choice that changed.

Useful distinctions: insects are animals. Red foxes and brown bears eat both plant and animal foods. Bear species have different diets. These groups describe the selected animals’ usual wild diets; they do not mean that every individual eats exactly the same things all the time.

Sources: each feedback card links to its animal profile from San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance or the University of Michigan’s Animal Diversity Web.
