    Vocabulary Help

    External force: a force applied from outside the bridge, such as weight, wind, or movement.

    Internal force: a force inside the bridge material as it reacts, such as tension, compression, shear, or bending.

    Engineer’s job: design a structure that spreads forces safely through the whole bridge.
