Build with point controls or a keyboard

Tap two grid points to connect them. Road pieces belong on the dashed road line. With the grid focused, use arrow keys to choose a point and Enter to connect; Escape cancels. Press 1 for Road, 2 for Wood, 3 for Steel, 4 for Erase. Beams meet only at their endpoints.

Try triangles, a different material or a shorter unsupported beam. Repeat the same test and compare peak stress, bending and cost in your notebook.

What does this model show?

The game calculates a 2D beam frame from your connections, stiffness, supports and moving axle loads. Strength, cost, wind and vehicle weight use relative classroom units. Stress combines bending and axial load; long compressed pieces have a lower buckling limit. The fall after a break is an animation. This is an investigation model, not a real bridge safety tool.

Saved designs and trials stay in this browser. Export your bridge or notebook to keep a separate copy. Crossing beams join only when they share an endpoint. Zoom helps on smaller screens.
