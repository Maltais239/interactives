Teacher notes & credits

Focus: describe typical seasonal changes in Alberta. These are observations, not rules for every place or every year. Snow can fall outside winter, and plants vary in when they grow or flower.

Earth’s tilted axis causes the yearly pattern of seasons and daylight. It is not caused by Earth being nearer to the Sun in summer.

Content reference: National Weather Service — Why Do We Have Seasons?.
