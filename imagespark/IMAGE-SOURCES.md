# Grade 7 image collection

Aligned to the user-supplied Social Studies 7–9 Curriculum workbook, Grade 7 sheet. Images are authentic source material, not generated reconstructions. Dates refer to the historical item unless stated otherwise. Scrip date is read from the printed document, not the digitization date. The 1884 Harris painting depicts 1864; the House of Commons photograph is from 2012.

Copies are resized to at most 1600 pixels and JPEG-compressed; no content is retouched or cropped by this app. Attribution and license links are also available beside every image.

Treaty texts represent a written Crown record. They do not replace First Nations oral histories or treaty understandings. Historical wording is retained in source documents; contemporary captions use current respectful terminology.
