Care Tips

  • Keep 💧 Hydration, ☀️ Warmth, and 🍖 Fullness (30% to below 90%) in the ideal range.
  • Too much water/warmth/food (>90%) contributes to 😟 Discomfort!
  • Low hydration/warmth/fullness (below 10%) also increases discomfort over time.
  • High discomfort (>75%) stops 😊 Happiness gain. Max discomfort (100%) is very bad!
  • Advance time when you are ready; observe how the needs change.
