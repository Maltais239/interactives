Read the right scale

The gold baseline starts at 0° on the right. Follow that scale around to 180° on the left.

90°A right angle points straight up.

Acute: less than 90°.
Right: exactly 90°.
Obtuse: between 90° and 180°.

A difference of up to 2° is accepted to allow for pointer precision. Hints and live measurement count as supported practice.
