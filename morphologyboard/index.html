Teacher notes and teaching references

The 52-word print collection remains available. These cards show one useful classroom analysis. Some words contain bound roots or several affixes; word parts are clues, and context refines the meaning. Spelling changes are stated explicitly. Ask students to use each word in a sentence.

How to Play

  1. Look at the solid color Word Card on the left.
  2. Use your magnetic letters to build the word on your cookie sheet board.
  3. Separate the word into its Prefix, Base/Root, and Suffix columns.
  4. Write the literal meaning at the bottom.
  5. Check your work against the Answer Card on the right!
