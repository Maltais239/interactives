Quick Tips

  • Keep 💧 Water (30% to below 90%) and ☀️ Sun (30% to below 90%) in the ideal range.
  • Keep 🌡️ Temperature ideally between 15°C and 30°C.
  • Too much water/sun (>90%) or extreme temperatures cause 😟 Stress!
  • Low water/sun (below 10%) also increases stress over time.
  • High stress (>75%) stops 🌱 Growth. At 100% stress, restart and compare a new trial.
  • Advance time when you are ready; observe how the levels change.

Care, observe, explain

This fictional classroom model lets you explore how care changes growth. Its percentages, time steps and ideal ranges are game rules; real plants have different needs.

Choose water/sunlight and temperature, predict what will happen, then advance one time step. Change one thing at a time for a fair comparison.
