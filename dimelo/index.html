How practice works · research & coverage

New words receive a learning step before recall. Correct, unaided answers return after a longer gap. Mistakes and supported answers return sooner. Recognizing, recalling, listening and use are tracked separately.

“Remembered later” needs successful unaided practice on separate days. A single correct answer does not finish an entry. The daily queue reserves places for unseen vocabulary, so new content keeps appearing alongside reviews.

Guided conversations check the phrases and response patterns in these lessons. They accept several responses and offer model replies when your meaning is outside those patterns. They are structured practice, rather than an unrestricted AI chat.

  • Kim & Webb (2022): spaced practice in second-language learning
  • Karatas et al.: retrieval, spacing and feedback for vocabulary
  • Lyster & Saito (2010): oral corrective feedback
  • de Jong & Perfetti (2011): repeated speaking tasks

These studies inform the practice design. The app itself has not undergone an effectiveness trial.
