Teacher notes & credits

Focus: explain how people transform materials. Here, processed means manufactured or substantially changed. Merely picking up or collecting a natural material does not change its category.

Useful pairs: raw cotton → cotton fabric; raw clay → fired brick; plant fibres → paper; iron ore → metal objects.
