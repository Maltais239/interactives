Cash rounding and classroom use

Canadian cash totals round to the nearest 5¢. Electronic payments keep the exact cent total. Starter mode shows the change due; practice and challenge modes ask you to work it out. Any correct combination is accepted. Tap or click money to add it, and select money in the tray to remove it. Keyboard users can use Tab, Enter and Space.
