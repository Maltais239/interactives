How latitude and longitude work

Latitude comes first.

Latitude tells how far north or south of the Equator a place is. Follow a horizontal line on this map.

Longitude comes second.

Longitude tells how far east or west of the Prime Meridian it is. Follow a vertical line.

This map uses the Mercator projection: land near the poles looks larger. The poles themselves are outside this map.
