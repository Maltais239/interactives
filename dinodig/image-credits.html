Image sources and reconstructions

Excavation images

The eight rock-embedded fossil images were generated with OpenAI image generation for STRATA. They are illustrative reconstructions based on fossil anatomy, not photographs of actual excavations or exact depictions of specimens from the map pins. Missing parts, arrangement and rock texture are interpreted. Use the written evidence records and museum research for real scientific findings.
