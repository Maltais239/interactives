For teachers · learning notes & image credits

Learning focus: connect resources and products to industries, then change the sorting rule. The second challenge distinguishes resources from the tools used to gather, process or deliver them.

Try this: sort once without clues, discuss a tricky card with a partner, then show field notes and revise. Ask, “What changed when the sorting rule changed?”

Card meanings: lumber is a product made from trees. “Oil in a drum” represents the oil inside the container. The fuel pump, fishing pole, axe, tractor and pickaxe are equipment, not natural resources. An object may connect with more than one industry in real life; these cards use the connection described in the field note.

Sources: Canada’s Open Science and Data Platform describes agriculture, fisheries, forestry, mining and energy projects. Canada’s natural resources pages and Natural Resources Canada’s minerals and metals facts provide further reading.

Artwork: the block-style cards come from the original BGSD sorting activity. The artwork has been resized and saved as individual files. This classroom activity is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.

Engaging Students · Explore. Explain. Revise.

Make a choice. Explain it. Revise it.

  1. Place a card. Drag with a mouse, or tap/click a card and choose a group. You can move a card again at any time.
  2. Look for evidence. Use “Show field notes” if you need a clue.
  3. Check and revise. Feedback explains each placed card. A “Revisit” label means there is more to think about.
  4. Try another challenge. Use the buttons above the board to switch challenges.

Keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter or Space to select it. Tab to a move button or group heading and press Enter. Arrow keys move between cards; Escape clears your selection.
